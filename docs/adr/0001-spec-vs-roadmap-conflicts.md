## Context

Both documents say that when they disagree, the Acceptance Specification decides (roadmap 3.1). This ADR records,
without resolving, every difference found while reading both in full, so the two of us agree on each one
explicitly instead of discovering it mid-build. Priority mapping used below: spec Critical = roadmap P0,
Important = P1, Conditional = P1 or P2 depending on whether the business uses the feature.

## Proposed default

**For every row: the Acceptance Specification wins**, and the roadmap is updated to match. Any row we decide
differently gets its own line in "Decision" with the reason. Rows marked _(owner)_ also need an answer from the
business owner (spec section 19.2).

### Registration liability

- MotoMart's current system shows registration as an item and a "processing fee" line on a bike sale.
- Spec section 7 (Registration/BRTA) is cancelled, but **A-10 and invariant 8 still apply**. Any amount
  collected on behalf of a third party, such as a BRTA fee, is a liability, never revenue.
- Only a fee that the showroom keeps as its own charge is revenue.
- Until the owner says what the processing fee is and where it goes, the vehicle sale treats it as a
  pass-through liability.

## Consequences

If the proposed default is accepted: Phase 1 tenancy schema adds a legal-entity level (row 1) and a restricted
NID field (row 2); Phase 3 grows by the accounting items in rows 3 to 5; Phase 2 and Phase 4 status machines follow
rows 6, 7, 10 and 11; the 56-week plan should be re-estimated at the Phase 0 gate.
