# Demo script, 29 Nov 2026

**For:** whoever runs the interview demo (Munif or Farhan). **Length:** about 15 minutes, plus questions.
**Based on:** the approved designs in `design/owner-dashboard/` (Main, Bikes, Inventory, Sales, and their
phone and dark versions) and the demo data in `tools/src/demo/` (#48). Every name, phone and number shown is
invented.

**The story in one line:** an owner of a two-business motorcycle group sees money and stock across four
branches; a cashier sells a bike; the owner's figures change, and the books stay balanced.

## Before the demo (30 minutes before)

1. Reset the demo database and run the seed (seed script: still to build, see "Status" at the end). It prints:
   - the totals the dashboard should show;
   - two unsold chassis numbers in Mirpur-10, for steps 6 and 8.

   Write them on a card.

2. `npm run dev`; open two browser windows side by side:
   - **left:** Owner (`owner@demo.test`);
   - **right:** Cashier (`cashier@demo.test`, Mirpur-10).

   Passwords come from `DEMO_PASSWORD`, set at seed time; they are never in the repo or on a slide.

3. Light theme, browser zoom 100%, English. Close every other tab. Turn notifications off.
4. Have a phone (or the browser's 390 px device view) ready for step 10.

## The script

Each step says what to click, what the screen shows, and the one sentence to say.

### 1. Sign in as the owner (left window)

- **Click:** type the owner's email and the password, **Sign in**.
- **Shows:** the owner dashboard; the header shows "Demo Group", all businesses, all branches.
- **Say:** "One login sees the whole group. A cashier's login sees one branch, and the database enforces that,
  not just the screen."

### 2. The owner dashboard (design: `Main.dc.html`)

- **Point at, top to bottom:**
  1. **Cash now:** cash, bank and bKash balances, read from the ledger.
  2. **Customers owe you**, split 0–30, 31–60 and 61+ days. The three parts add up to the total, because both
     come from the same books.
  3. **Revenue by branch** and **profit by branch**: Mirpur-10, Uttara, Agrabad, Narayanganj.
  4. **Revenue and expenses** by month: September to November, from the seeded sales.
  5. **As of** time and **Refresh**.
- **Say:** "Every figure here is a query over balanced journals. Nothing is typed in by hand, so the dashboard
  can't disagree with the accounts."

### 3. Switch business and branch (header)

- **Click:** the business switcher → **Demo Parts**; then back to **All businesses**; then the branch
  → **Mirpur-10**.
- **Shows:** every card changes to that scope; the header always shows where you are.
- **Say:** "Each request carries the business it works in. A tab can't write into the wrong business by
  accident."

### 4. Products by chassis (design: `Bikes.dc.html`)

- **Click:** **Inventory → Products**; search the first chassis number from the card.
- **Shows:** the unit, its model and its status (**In stock**), branch Mirpur-10. The **Models** list shows
  available and sold counts per model.
- **Say:** "Every bike is tracked by its own chassis number, from the supplier to the customer."

### 5. Stock on hand (design: `Inventory.dc.html`)

- **Click:** **Inventory**; **All branches**, then **Mirpur-10**; search "helmet".
- **Shows:** quantity per branch with lakh grouping; a part with no stock says **Out of stock** in words, not
  just in colour.
- **Say:** "On hand is the sum of stock movements. It is never typed in, and it can't go below zero."

### 6. Sell a bike (right window, cashier; design: `Sales.dc.html`)

- **Click:**
  1. **Sales / POS**; customer: pick a seeded customer (e.g. "Rahim Traders").
  2. Add the bike model; **pick the chassis** from the card.
  3. Add a helmet.
  4. **Payment:** part in **Cash**, the rest by **bKash** with a transaction id (e.g. `BK-20261129-0001`).
  5. **Save**.
- **Shows:** the invoice number and the receipt number, generated by the system; **Balance due** is zero.
- **Say:** "That one click wrote the invoice, took the bike out of stock, and posted a balanced journal, all in
  one database transaction. If any part fails, none of it happens. A retry after a network drop can't charge
  twice."

### 7. The owner sees it (left window)

- **Click:** **Refresh** on the dashboard.
- **Shows:** **Cash now** and Mirpur-10's revenue go up by the amounts just taken; the bike's unit now says
  **Sold**.
- **Say:** "Same data, no export, no overnight job."

### 8. Two cashiers, one chassis (both windows)

- **Click:** in both windows, as cashier, put the **second** chassis from the card in a sale; press **Save** in
  both, as close together as you can.
- **Shows:** one succeeds; the other says the unit is no longer available, and nothing of that sale is saved.
- **Say:** "The database decides who wins. The loser's invoice, stock and journal all roll back together."

### 9. A cashier can't see another branch

- **Click:** right window (cashier, Mirpur-10): open the branch switcher.
- **Shows:** only Mirpur-10. Optionally paste the URL of an Uttara invoice: **not found**.
- **Say:** "Row-level security in PostgreSQL filters every query by the caller's businesses and branches. An
  automated test tries this on every route."

### 10. Bangla and phone

- **Click:** language → **বাংলা**; then the phone view.
- **Shows:** the dashboard in Bangla with Bangla digits (৳ ১২,৩৪,৫৬৭.০০); on a phone, one column, figures
  first, tables as cards.
- **Say:** "Built for the people at the counter and the owner on the move."

### 11. Close: what is not in this slice

**Say plainly** (plan section 7):

- **Not in this slice:** approvals, the full activity record, hiding cost and profit from roles that shouldn't
  see them, attachments, WhatsApp and email messages.
- **Next:** these come in the next two weeks (Phase B).

## If something breaks

- **A screen errors:** press **Try again** (every screen has it); if it persists, move to the next step and say
  which one you skipped.
- **The race in step 8 doesn't show** (both saves too far apart): explain it, and show the integration test
  that proves it (`npm run test:integration`, the parallel-sale test).
- **Login fails:** use the prepared second browser profile that is already signed in.
